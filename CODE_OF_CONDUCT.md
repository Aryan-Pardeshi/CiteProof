# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want CiteProof to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code review.
- Assume good faith and ask questions before assuming mistakes.
- Give credit, and accept feedback on your own work gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the technical merits.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam or unsolicited promotion in issues and pull requests.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the links on the [author's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Aryan-Pardeshi).

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
